## Customer privacy before publication

Every contributor and agent must anonymize private customer context before creating or updating public PR titles, bodies, comments and reviews; branch names and commit messages; or committed code, tests, fixtures and documentation. Never copy private customer data from support threads, incident investigations, logs or connected sources into this repository.

Remove customer names; organization, repository, domain, tenant and account identifiers; identifying keys, paths and log excerpts; and private customer job, support or incident links. Credentials, known tokens and secrets are private. Use neutral wording, synthetic data and minimal reproducible examples.

Check screenshot and recording pixels, captions and filenames before publishing. Use synthetic reproductions or keep identifying evidence private. Publishing first and scrubbing later is unacceptable.

Preserve this repository's public identity, dependencies and intentionally public attributions, including public authors and third-party software. Do not add a real customer-name blacklist.

If you find an existing inadvertent disclosure, stop propagating it and flag it privately for authorized cleanup. Do not delete content or rewrite history without approval.
